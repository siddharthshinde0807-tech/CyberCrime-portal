Public Advisory

Protect Yourself Against Cyber Fraud

Recognize common digital scams and learn immediate action steps to safeguard your financial and personal data.

Emergency National Cyber Helpline

Dial 1930 immediately to freeze unauthorized bank transactions within the golden hour.

Financial Fraud

Never share OTPs, UPI PINs, or debit card CVV codes. Bank officials will never ask for secret passwords over phone calls.

Phishing & Link Scams

Avoid clicking suspicious links sent via WhatsApp or SMS promising lottery wins, job offers, or electricity bill urgency.

Identity Theft

Enable Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) on all social media accounts. Never post identity documents publicly online.

File an Incident Report
